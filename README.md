This is the main page for the company, AwareAI.

AwareAI is a health tech company that created a smart app for your smartwatch to help people in their daily lives. The company uses AI to track things like health, stress, sleep, and activity patterns. Their goal is to make technology simple, helpful, and easy for everyone to use.
